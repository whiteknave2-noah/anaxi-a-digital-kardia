## What the closing work taught

A capability does not become established because a backend exists, a single fixture passes, or a retrieval step succeeds. The **ordinary production path** must connect source identity, authority, privacy, actual delivery, persistence, recovery, truthful failure, and meaningful affordance. The final retrospective showed why repairs must cover the *demonstrated failure class*, including its genuinely affected sources and paths, rather than only the convenient observed example. Anything outside the evidence remains **NOT_ESTABLISHED**.

This requirement is bounded: discovering another conceivable uncertainty does not automatically earn another engineering project.

## Whole-occasion budget and correspondence

Context planning must budget not just the requested historical payload but its necessary source/provenance carriers, authority framing, and completion room. ANAXI qualified a **47,616-token serving context** with whole-occasion accounting. Mandatory provenance is not optional overhead.

Authenticated correspondence also distinguishes the exact incoming human source from the host-rendered presentation used in model context. Canonical persistence must validate the appropriate source, not assume the two are byte-identical. Recovery preserves the **one canonical human event → at most one canonical subject response** invariant; interruption must not cause fabricated human speech or duplicate responses.
